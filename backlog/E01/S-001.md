## Story
As a visitor, I want to browse the site by year, so that I can see the 10 best movies for that year.

## Origin
PRD story block, `**Added.**` marker. /ideate.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **AC1.** When a visitor opens the site, the system shows a list of years that have a published top-10 list.
- [x] **AC2.** When a visitor selects a year, the system shows that year's 10 movies in rank order.
- [x] **AC3.** When a year has no published list yet, the system does not show it in the year list.

## Out of scope
Editing the list, movie detail beyond title, poster, and rank.

## Context
PRD: E01 / S-001. DESIGN: Home/Years, Year page. ARCHITECTURE: Next.js App Router + Prisma, `code/frontend`.

## Plan

Goal-backward from the three criteria. End state: a visitor opens the real site in a browser, sees a grid of only the years whose `published` flag is true (AC1, AC3), clicks one, and lands on that year's page showing its movies ordered by `rank` ascending (AC2). Nothing here writes data or touches auth, so it is unattended and stays in sketch mode's lighter lane.

What already exists (checked, do not re-create): the full Prisma schema (`code/frontend/prisma/schema.prisma`) including `Year.published` and `Movie.@@unique([yearId, rank])`, the Prisma client singleton (`src/lib/db.ts`), a placeholder `src/app/page.tsx`, and a working Playwright harness (`playwright.config.ts` builds and starts the app). What is missing: an actual migration, the read queries, the two screens, their components, and the tests.

Two identifiers are pinned so every layer agrees: the published-years query is `getPublishedYears()` and the single-year query is `getYearWithMovies(year: number)`, both in `src/lib/years.ts`. The year route is keyed by the integer `year` value (`/years/[year]`), not the cuid id, so URLs read `/years/1994` per DESIGN 3.2.

Enforcement of the criteria lives in the query layer, not just the UI: `getPublishedYears` filters `where: { published: true }` (AC1, AC3) and orders `year` descending (DESIGN 3.1 "most recent first"); `getYearWithMovies` returns null for an unpublished or absent year and orders movies `rank` ascending (AC2). The Home empty state (no published years) is a legitimate first-run state per DESIGN 3.1, not an error.

ui_surface is true: all three criteria are observed through real screens and the year link is a real click, which Playwright can drive automatically, so the story stays unattended with a durable e2e test rather than supervised.

`ui_surface: true`. The durable end-to-end proof is the Playwright spec in T6, which seeds published and unpublished years plus a ten-movie year, then drives the real browser: asserts the unpublished year is absent (AC3) and published years are present (AC1), clicks a year card, and asserts the ten movies render in rank order (AC2).
